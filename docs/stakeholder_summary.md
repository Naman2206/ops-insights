# Plain-Language Summary (for non-technical stakeholders)

**What we looked at:** about 70,000 orders processed across four centers (Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Bengaluru) from January 2025 to June 2026, pulled from two different systems.

**What we found**

1. **Work is growing fast.** Daily orders are up roughly 46% compared with early 2025. Monday is by far the busiest day; Sunday is the quietest.
2. **One clear bottleneck.** Verification at the Delhi center takes about 9 hours on average, versus about 5 hours at other centers. Nearly half of its orders miss the 8-hour target.
3. **Busy days hurt quality.** On the heaviest days the error rate roughly doubles (2.7% to 5.5%).
4. **Mondays run slow.** Orders take about an hour longer on Mondays because of weekend backlog.

**What we recommend**

- Add or rebalance Verification staff in Delhi first. It is the largest single source of missed targets.
- Schedule extra capacity on Mondays and on forecasted peak days rather than spreading staff evenly.
- Use the 14-day forecast in the dashboard as an early signal when planning rosters.

**How reliable is the forecast?** On the last 28 days of data it was off by about 8% per day on average, slightly better than simply assuming "same as last week". It captures trend and weekday pattern, but not holidays or promotions, so treat it as a planning guide, not a guarantee.

**About the data:** about 2% of raw records had problems (duplicates, missing completion times, impossible timestamps) and were excluded. Every excluded record is logged with a reason.
